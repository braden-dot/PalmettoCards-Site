Our Story

Collectors Serving Collectors

Palmetto Cards was born from a shared passion for the hobby. We're a team of collectors, enthusiasts, and sports fans who decided to open the kind of card shop we always wished existed in South Carolina.

Located in the heart of downtown Lexington, SC, we're your local destination for buying, selling, trading, and breaking sports cards. We treat every collection with respect and every customer like family.

From your first $1 common to a high-dollar rookie auto, we're here for every level of the hobby. Come see us — we'd love to talk cards.
